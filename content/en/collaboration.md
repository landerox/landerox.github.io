# :material-hand-heart: Collaboration

> I join engineering teams as an embedded senior engineer — in their pull
> requests, Slack and architecture reviews — to design, build and automate
> data, cloud and AI systems.

What teams usually bring me in for, by area:

<div class="service-list reading-panel" markdown="block">

* **Applied AI & MLOps.** Taking an agent or RAG prototype to production with
  evaluation, telemetry and cost limits, and automating ML deployment.

* **Cloud & Platform Engineering.** Landing zones, zero-trust CI/CD and
  policy checks defined as code (Terraform / OpenTofu, GitHub Actions, OPA
  Rego), plus cloud cost reviews (FinOps).

* **Data Engineering & Pipelines.** Building or stabilizing batch and
  streaming pipelines (Spark, dbt, Airflow, Dagster), with tests, backfills
  and recovery.

* **Data & Storage Architecture.** Dimensional models, data contracts and
  the choice between a data warehouse and an open-table lakehouse.

</div>

Recent client work spans **Banking and finance**, **Retail**, and **Energy** —
operating under strict regulatory, security, and data isolation constraints.

<div class="reading-panel" markdown="block">

## Working arrangements

**Engagements.** I work as an independent contractor, on a project basis:
from a short architecture audit or design review (2–4 weeks) to several
months as an embedded technical lead or senior engineer (3–6+ months),
including code review and mentoring.

**Clouds.** My deepest experience is on Google Cloud; I also design and
deploy on AWS regularly.

**Time zones.** I work from Caracas (UTC-4, no daylight saving), which
overlaps the business day across the Americas and European late mornings
and afternoons. For that overlap I can meet between **05:00 and 22:00
Caracas time**; the availability pill on the home page shows a typical day.

</div>
